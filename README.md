# Tunnelo.NET

**[tunnelo.net](https://tunnelo.net)** — a privacy-focused VPN for people who want simple, honest access without giving up their identity at the door.

---

## What is Tunnelo?

Tunnelo encrypts your internet connection and routes it through our network so websites, apps, and your provider see less about what you do online. We built it for everyday privacy: browsing, streaming, travel, and getting around unfair blocks — not for collecting data about you.

---

## How we’re different

**Anonymous accounts**  
You don’t need an email to sign up. You get a random account number and a recovery phrase. That’s how you log in and get your time back if you lose access — not a marketing profile tied to your inbox.

**Prepaid time, no subscription trap**  
You buy VPN time upfront (for example one, six, or twelve months). It runs down on your account. There’s no silent auto-renewal charging your card every month unless you choose to add time again.

**Privacy by design**  
We aim to collect as little as possible. Our [no-logs policy](https://tunnelo.net/no-logs) explains what we don’t keep on VPN servers. Legal details are in our [privacy policy](https://tunnelo.net/privacy).

**WireGuard**  
Tunnelo uses modern WireGuard connections — fast to set up on phones and computers, and flexible enough for routers and advanced setups if you need them.

**Pay how you want**  
Add time with common payment options (card, PayPal, crypto, vouchers, and more, depending on region and rollout).
