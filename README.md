## What Tunnelo is

Tunnelo sends your traffic through an encrypted tunnel so your ISP and random sites see less of what you do online. You pay for time on your account, connect with our apps or WireGuard, and leave. We do not try to turn you into a long-term data profile.

If you only remember one thing: **you get a number and a recovery phrase, not another inbox login.**

Prices move with deals. The table is about **how the product works**, not a price scraper. Tunnelo Personal on a 12 month pack works out to **about €3 per month**. Big brands often look cheap on the first year, then bill email accounts on renewal.

## Why people switch

**Less signup data.** No email means one less link between your real identity and your VPN account.

**Prepaid is simple.** You see the time on your account. You add more when you choose. No surprise renewal if you forgot to toggle a checkbox.

**Payments that match privacy.** Card, PayPal, crypto, vouchers, and more in the dashboard, depending on what we have live in your region.

**WireGuard everywhere it matters.** Phones, desktops, and configs when you want to roll your own.
